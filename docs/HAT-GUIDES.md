# One page per role

Everyone signs in with their email address and a code that is emailed to them. A volunteer's **hat** (set by an administrator under Users)
decides what they see. Members see only their own account.

## Treasurer
* **Finance > Invoices**: raise invoices (price list, quantities, nominal codes), email or print them, record payments, issue credit notes.
  Void or write off with a reason; both are in the audit log.
* **Finance > Bank**: upload the bank statement; the office matches payments to invoices. Lines that are not invoice payments are categorised as income, an
  expense, a transfer or bank charges (rules remember repeat items).
* **Finance > Reports**: debtors, income by nominal compared with last year, receipts and payments, bank reconciliation.
* **Finance > Year end**: check nothing is left open, download the examiner's pack, close the books.
* Online payments: GoCardless Direct Debit first, then card; bank transfer always works.

## Membership secretary
* **New members**: enquiries arrive from the website; vet them, ask for what is missing, approve. Payment comes after vetting and starts the
  membership.
* **Members / Contacts**: records, families and who pays, tags and lists. **Season** runs the yearly renewal: tell members, bill them, chase.
* **Mailings**: write to a list; opted-out people are left out automatically.

## Harbourmaster (boats and berths)
* **Boats**: register, owners, insurance dates and reminders. **Storage**: spaces, allocations, the waiting list, tariffs and the storage billing run.

## Training officer
* **Training**: courses, bookings, waiting lists, attendance, outcomes, qualifications and junior consent.

## Social secretary
* **Events**: a calendar of what is on. Make an event, tick *Tickets can be bought*, add ticket types (members, the public, free or priced) and
  publish it. People buy from the **What's on** page; visitors need no account and are emailed an invoice with a private pay link. Add duty slots
  (bar, kitchen); members take them from their own page and are reminded before the day. Export the attendee list, tick people off on arrival,
  copy last year's event to next year.


## Administrator
* **Users**: who has which hat. **Jobs**: what runs by itself, rehearse or run now. **Audit**: who changed what.
* Keep at least two administrators.
