How we look after your information

{{ club }} is a members' club. We keep the details you give us (name, contact details, date of birth, boats, qualifications, payments) so that we can run your membership, send you invoices and keep everyone safe on the water.

What we do with it

  • Run your membership, bookings and boat storage, and send you invoices and reminders.
  • Contact you about the club, if you have said we may.
  • Keep the financial records the law requires us to keep.

Who sees it

Only the volunteers who need it for their role. We do not sell or share your details. Details about children are only collected from a parent or guardian.

Your rights

You can see and correct your details after signing in, download a copy of everything we hold, or ask us to erase you. Financial records (invoices and payments) are kept for six years without your name attached once you are erased.

Enquiries and applications

If you ask a question or apply and do not join, we keep your enquiry for a limited time so we can follow up, then remove it.

Questions? Use the contact form.
